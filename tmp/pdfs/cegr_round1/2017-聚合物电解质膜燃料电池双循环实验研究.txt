Article history:                                   Durability and start-up ability in sub-zero environment are two technical bottlenecks of
Received 21 February 2017                          vehicular polymer electrolyte membrane (PEM) fuel cell systems. With exhaust gas recir-
Received in revised form                           culation on the anode and cathode side, the cell voltage at low current density can be
1 April 2017                                       reduced, and the membrane can be humidified without external humidifier. They may be
Accepted 18 April 2017                             helpful to prolong the working lifetime and to promote the start-up ability. This paper
Available online 10 May 2017                       presents an experimental study on a PEM fuel cell system with anodic and cathodic
                                                   recirculation. The system is built up based on a 10 kW fuel cell stack, which consists of
Keywords:                                          50 cells and has an active area of 261 cm2. A cathodic recirculation pump and a hydrogen
Polymer electrolyte membrane fuel                  recirculation pump are utilized on the cathode and anode side, respectively. Key param-
cell                                               eters, e.g., stack current, stack voltage, cell voltage, air flow, relative humidity on the
Anodic recirculation                               cathode side, oxygen concentration at the inlet and outlet of the cathode side, are
Cathodic recirculation                             measured. Results show that: 1) with a cathodic recirculation the system gets good self-
Voltage clamping                                   humidification effect, which is similar to that with an external humidifier; 2) with a
Self-humidification                                cathodic recirculation and a reduction of fresh air flux, the cell voltage can be obviously
Durability                                         reduced; 3) with an anodic recirculation the cell voltage can also be reduced due to a
                                                   reduction in the hydrogen partial pressure, the relative humidity on the cathode side is a
                                                   little smaller than the case with only cathode recirculation. It indicates that, for our stack
                                                   the cathodic recirculation is effective to clamp cell voltage at low current density, and a
                                                   self-humidification system is possible with cathodic recirculation. Further study will focus
                                                   on the dynamic model and control of the dual recirculation fuel cell system.
